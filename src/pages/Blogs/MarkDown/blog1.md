# My Experience Interviewing for the AWS Solutions Architect Intern Position at Amazon

I recently interviewed for an **AWS Solutions Architect Intern** position at **Amazon** and wanted to share my experience and preparation process. I hope this blog post will help others preparing for similar interviews.

## Interview Process

The interview process consisted of **two rounds** with **back-to-back interviews**:

1. **First Interview** – Conducted by a **Senior Solutions Architect**
2. **Second Interview** – Conducted by another **Solutions Architect**

Both interviews focused on **behavioral and technical** aspects, as Amazon places a **strong emphasis on leadership principles** alongside technical expertise.

---

## Behavioral Questions

Amazon's interview process includes **at least two behavioral questions** tied to their **Leadership Principles**.

Some of the questions I was asked included:

- _"Tell me about a time when you had to deal with a difficult team member. How did you handle the situation?"_
- _"How do you prioritize your work and manage your time effectively?"_

📌 **Tip:** Use the **STAR Method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure your responses clearly and effectively.

---

## Technical Questions

The **technical portion** of the interview focused on **cloud computing concepts, AWS services, and architecture design**. Some of the questions I encountered included:

### Cloud Computing Concepts

- Differences between **IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service), PaaS (Platform as a Service), and SaaS (Software as a Service)**.
- Benefits of using **cloud computing** over traditional infrastructure.

### AWS Services & Architecture

- How would you **secure** an application deployed on AWS?
- What are the **different EC2 instance types**, and when would you use each?
- How does **Amazon S3** provide high availability and durability?
- When should you use **RDS** versus **DynamoDB**?
- How does **AWS Lambda** handle scaling?

---

## My Preparation Strategy

To prepare for the interview, I focused on **three key areas**:

### 1. AWS Services & Documentation

- Studied **AWS Whitepapers** and **AWS Well-Architected Framework**.
- Reviewed official **AWS documentation** for EC2, S3, RDS, IAM, and Lambda.
- Practiced AWS **hands-on labs** using **AWS Free Tier**.

### 2. Behavioral Interviews

- Studied Amazon's **Leadership Principles** and crafted personal stories for each.
- Practiced answering behavioral questions using the **STAR method**.

### 3. Mock Interviews & Online Courses

- Watched **YouTube tutorials** on AWS interview prep.
- Took **cloud computing courses** on **Udemy** and **Coursera**.
- Conducted **mock interviews** with peers and mentors.

---

## Final Thoughts

The **AWS Solutions Architect Intern interview** was **challenging but rewarding**. It deepened my understanding of **AWS services** and **cloud architecture** while improving my **problem-solving skills**.

### 💡 Key Takeaways

✔ Review **AWS documentation** and try hands-on labs.  
✔ Practice **behavioral questions** using the **STAR method**.  
✔ Understand **AWS architectural best practices**.  
✔ Take advantage of **online courses and mock interviews**.

🚀 If you're preparing for this interview, I highly recommend starting early, practicing consistently, and **staying confident**.

**Best of luck!** 💡🔥
